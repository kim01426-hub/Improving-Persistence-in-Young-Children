Simplified Horizons Task

Web port of TimeSearch_190603.py (Zhuang, Niebaum & Munakata, 2023). Runs offline in the browser, on a computer or an iPad. To advance an instruction screen, press Space or tap the bottom-right corner. Long-press the top-left corner for 1.5 s to save and review data at any point.

Correct splash aspect ratio Original stretched every splash ~1.6× horizontally (a side effect of PsychoPy norm units). Unchecked = reproduce the stretch.
Equalise ink area across colours Original scaled the image box, not the paint. Same reward value looked up to 1.8× bigger in dark blue than in red. Unchecked = reproduce that difference.
Include the 5 practice trials

Data downloads as CSV at the end and is also shown on screen so it can be copied. On iPad, turn on Guided Access first (Settings › Accessibility) so the child cannot swipe out of the task.
